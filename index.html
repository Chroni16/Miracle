File 01 — Profile

The short version

Four disciplines, one working method: research thoroughly, build carefully, communicate clearly.

I started out studying history and diplomacy — learning how to read a source critically, argue a position calmly, and manage a room that disagrees with you. That training turned out to be excellent preparation for project management.

I'm currently a Warehouse IT Support Analyst with Field Technology Development Partners, supporting the systems and tech infrastructure behind warehouse operations. Before that, I was a Project Manager at Shoppers Warehouse in Lagos, running E-commerce projects end-to-end — from electronics sales outlets and warehouse construction to web platform rollouts. Earlier still, I handled sourcing and procurement across electronics, FMCG, and general merchandise, and managed facility operations and asset control.

Across every role, the thread is the same: organize the complexity, protect the details, and communicate clearly with whoever's in the room — vendor, executive, or team.
